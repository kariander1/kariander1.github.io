Shai Yehezkel

I am a M.Sc. student in Computer Science at Tel Aviv University, supervised by Prof. Daniel Cohen-Or. My research focuses on machine learning, computer vision and computer graphics.

As of summer 2025, I am also a research intern at Apple in Herzliya.

I graduated with a B.Sc. in Computer Engineering from the Technion (Cum Laude). Previously, I worked as an Algorithm & System Engineer at Vayyar Imaging, a Software Student at Intel, and a Software Developer at SpaceIL.

Love coding, and passionate about space!
